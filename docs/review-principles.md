## Continuous work and confirmations

One large agreed task without artificial stops. After approval, perform exactly the approved write, inspect saved content/diff and continue preparing the next agreed package without waiting for «продолжай». Each write requires its own exact approval. A general «продолжай» with no pending concrete write is not blanket permission.

The owner may not see a separate confirmation UI. End the ordinary Russian response with a plain-language summary of what changed, what comes next and a clear confirmation question naming the exact files/scope. Do not rely on an invisible prompt or make the owner manage technical debugging. Stop for required confirmation, changed scope, consequential ambiguity, missing owner materials or a real blocker. Never promise background work. ZIP in the old owner rule was voice-input error, not a required archive workflow.
